# Ice-cream evidence and automation handoff

The buyer needs strictly chocolate ice cream delivered frozen to Nha Trang. Compare each selected selling option independently by VND per gram of food, per millilitre, and per gram of protein, before and after delivery. [Current separate rankings](tables/ice-unit-rankings.md) and their [JSON](tables/ice-unit-rankings.json) preserve partial coverage instead of inventing missing quantities.

## Immediate evidence workflow

1. Finish the relevant category/list pass before product detail work. Preserve every candidate and exclusion with its reason; never apply a package-price ceiling that could hide a cheap bulk offer.
2. Resolve the exact listing and selected SKU, flavour, package count, availability, discounted sale price and original price. Factory carton count is independent of the selected selling count. Preserve the observation timestamp and source hash.
3. Identify the manufacturer from original gallery, barcode and description. Search its official site for the exact brand, chocolate core, package and market revision. Keep alternate names in Vietnamese, English, Korean and Thai as associative aliases; never merge products on a translated name alone.
4. Cache original manufacturer HTML/JSON/YAML and package images before extraction. Reuse cached originals, OCR text, page snapshots and review decisions. OCR the original resolution; visually compare each quantitative fact against its exact source crop. A marketing front without a back label is insufficient for nutrition.
5. Store grams and millilitres as separate facts. Accept both printed package quantities or a manufacturer-declared density for that exact formulation. Never use1g=1ml, a generic ice-cream density, or volume-to-weight guesses. Serving mass is package mass only when the full-container serving count is explicitly confirmed.
6. Store nutrition with its explicit basis: per package, per100g, per100ml or a stated serving. Protein per100ml can establish a protein denominator without package grams. Retain energy, protein, carbohydrate, sugar, fat, saturated fat, sodium, ingredients and allergens when disclosed. Absence is a source-specific gap; carbohydrate is not sugar.
7. Match evidence identity field by field. Source priority is the exact manufacturer package/specification, followed by an exact current original label from another source, then the seller declaration. A historical sample report does not prove a current formulation. Preserve conflicting values and require a checked rationale for their resolution.
8. Price the actual quantity with eligible bulk tiers and confirmed fixed-order discounts applied once. Record destination and quantity with freight. Require confirmed frozen delivery for ice cream; ambient shipping or HCM express wording cannot establish Nha Trang cold-chain availability. Keep unverified checkout evidence in the private overlay.
9. Generate independent ascending rankings for each denominator. Exclude sold-out and confirmed wrong-flavour selections from buying rankings. Retain their source observations in the audit. Incomplete items remain in a resumable worklist, outside only the ranking that requires their missing fact.

## Resumable associative data model

Persist product identity, selected SKU, source capture, raw blob, OCR run, original-language text, translation, field claim, identity review, correction, nutrition basis, offer observation, discount condition and delivery quote as separate linked records. Content hashes identify unchanged bytes. Each corrected fact links to its original source and superseded claims; invalidation targets only affected extraction and derived metrics.

Archive reproduction exposed a metadata ambiguity: plain capture objects containing `sha256` and `bytes` are interpreted as stored blob references. Historical pre-redaction digests can then fail export even when the sanitized source is present. The research producer stores those provenance fields as `contentSha256` and `byteLength`, retaining the original JSON separately. A typed blob-reference schema and regression for source-rewrite metadata remain tracked in [archive issue#5](https://github.com/konard/lazada-search/issues/5).

Manufacturer research must use a shared URL/domain scheduler and a durable job queue. Cache successful captures and failed attempts with timestamps and errors. Honor at least60seconds between Lazada requests, reuse the persistent browser, wait for product content/loading completion, and resolve dialogs before navigation or extraction. No repeated speculative URL polling. Browser restarts reuse the profile and window; the existing30-minute idle-close preference remains.

The urgent report module accepts reviewed numeric facts only when they carry exact-identity approval, source URL, SHA-256 and an original excerpt. It calculates package-, mass- and volume-based protein independently and flags contradictory bases. It currently feeds the report; integration of volume/package protein into the library, CLI and Telegram is a required next step, not completed interface support.
