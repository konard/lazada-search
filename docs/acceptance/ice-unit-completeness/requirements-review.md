# Ice unit completeness and remaining requirements review

This offline audit reviewed the saved [requirements](../../REQUIREMENTS.md), [current acceptance snapshot](../requirements-audit/current.json), [resume checklist](../../RESUME.md), calculation library and local interface tests after the user resumed work. It made zero marketplace, manufacturer or CDN requests. The persisted manufacturer CAPTCHA stop remains active.

## Initial concrete findings

The calculation library already prices grams and millilitres independently when both are known: `calculateOffer` produces both before/after rate pairs from the measured selling-unit quantities in [compare.js](../../../src/compare.js). [Carton E2E](../../../tests/e2e/ice-selling-units.test.js) verifies that 24 bottles of 131 g / 130 ml ordered twice produce 6,288 g / 6,240 ml and correctly distinct unit rates. No assumed ice-cream density is necessary.

The original Telegram formatting hid those known rates for unverified observations. Its captured-price section showed only the chosen sorting metric, and its pending section described every specification as unknown even when volume was known. That presentation defect is fixed in [telegram.js](../../../src/telegram.js): verified, unverified captured and pending rows now share the same quantities and all before/after unit rates. Verification status and eligibility problems remain explicit. A row sorted by grams can still display its independently known millilitre rate; unknown freight retains an unknown delivered rate.

The initial published reports contained a separate propagation gap: the two Binggrae report rows had source-reviewed 131 g / 130 ml package facts, but the general catalog products still had `netMassG: null`. The report-only enrichment in [ice-unit-rankings.mjs](../../../scripts/ice-unit-rankings.mjs) did not itself update the shared [comparison inputs](../../../src/comparison-inputs.js). Relevant exact SKUs are `2978009544_VNAMZ-14350953081` and `3040048563_VNAMZ-14623760583`. The saved actual-carton E2E explicitly expected missing mass before this follow-up. A new durable [package-quantity review](../../../src/package-quantity-review.js) now applies validated printed grams/millilitres through [catalog products](../../../src/catalog-products.js), which feeds library, CLI, HTTP and Telegram comparisons. Its saved record is bound to the exact product/SKU, observation date, selection, original listing HTML and attached label bytes. The actual public-case importer has applied the two original PowerCap reviews without changing prices or observation dates.

The printed 131 g approval is limited to the matched export wrapper. Its [review](../ice-dual-units/root-expanded-review.json) retains the Brazilian secondary host, source SHA-256, exact per-package basis and lot/formula limitations. Seller nutrition is a reference fact and cannot become manufacturer verification. The manufacturer nutrition endpoint contains placeholders rather than valid zero nutrients. The shared review accepts only positive grams/millilitres declared for one inner food package. It adds attributed manual-review claims without approving nutrition or factory identity, preserves explicit holds, and withdraws its derived quantity if any original binding, source bytes or active invalidation becomes unusable. A private overlay can reuse the exact public capture review while preserving its own price. Identical validated reviews reuse their durable record.
