## Ambiguities retained for collection

The ProSupps listing mixes Premium 28-serving packaging and older Advanced Leaning packaging advertising L-carnitine and 23/24 servings. Its current official premium label is cached, but those mixed seller images do not establish which formulation will ship. That listing remains unverified.

Four [Optimum Nutrition factory flavour selections](../optimum-nutrition-labels.json) retain complete browser HTML and their exact published nutrition-image references. Current factory packages include 29-serving 1.98 lb chocolate/vanilla, 29-serving 2 lb strawberry and 71-serving 5 lb Extreme Milk Chocolate. Several Lazada selections instead claim rounded 2 lb or 80-serving 5.5 lb packages, and some are repacks. A direct factory label-image request returned HTTP 403; no repeated failing requests were made. Formula and package differences remain explicit rather than applying another region's or batch's label.

Aice's official chocolate-tub page currently publishes a front image for a ten-piece chocolate popsicle box. Its other tub images have very small nutrition print. The collected HTML, images and OCR are preserved, but these sources do not establish the exact Lazada tubs' mass, formula or protein content. Happy Gelato's Vietnamese website could not be reached. These gaps require better source evidence.

All captured shipping quotes apply to one package. Bulk quantities require an actual destination-specific cart quote, and frozen products also require confirmed frozen transport.
