## Implemented in this checkpoint

Selected offer/SKU/quantity, source bytes/URL/time, actual destination, visible fields and checkout arithmetic are checked. Merchandise and shipping discounts are capped independently and duplicate voucher fields reject. Prices, shipping and already-applied discounts count once per exact basket; generic one-package freight is not multiplied into a bulk quote.

Capture uses the persistent private page, resolves dialogs/loading, waits for stable totals, aborts if the source URL changes and timestamps the observed final HTML. It respects persistent CAPTCHA stops and performs no navigation, cart preparation, voucher application or purchase. Comparison applies shared category/manufacturer reviews, specification holds, original availability and invalidation safeguards. Destination mismatch, stale sources, absent/negated frozen delivery, wrong SKU, stock/quantity constraints and missing corresponding denominators prevent accepted rankings. Grams, millilitres and protein remain independent quantities. Reports explicitly declare `captured-exact-baskets-only` and `allBasketCombinationsCollected=false`.

[Food-component accounting](../../../src/food-components.js) separates paid food, bonus food and accessories. Exact reviewed identity, package/formula/market, order quantity, eligibility, stock, limits and nutrient bases gate bonus inclusion; potential gifts remain visible without inflated denominators. Paid multipacks and bonus allocations cannot count twice. Invalid component records reject before import/quote mutation, and typed metadata survives offer history, restart and associative conversion. A remaining stored-original verification safeguard is assigned for integration: metadata checks alone do not establish that an accessible non-invalidated original contains the asserted digest, URL, excerpt and exact factory authority.

[Cached translation](../../translation.md) now checks source numbers, unit pairs, nutrition bases, codes, protected entities and supported negative conditions on generation and replay. Unsuitable results remain pending without usable English; checker/source/output changes invalidate older review. Human review binds exact original/translated hashes and provider/checker identity, and cannot approve failed quantity/entity checks. Source facts stay authoritative.

The [presentation helper](../../../src/translation-presentation.js) copies reviewed cached English into product titles, descriptions, ingredient positions and selected variants, including `bestByCategory` winners, while retaining original Vietnamese and all metrics. Telegram and the local web calculator consume the overlay. Resumable catalog translation remains cache-only by default and distinguishes successful translations from reviewed acceptance. Complete reviewed-chunk reassembly and commercial offer-text presentation are assigned follow-ups, pending integration/tests at this inspection. Private destinations, addresses, phone/email and credential fields must remain excluded from provider planning.
