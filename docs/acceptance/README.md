[Published catalog tables](../tables/README.md) are the current readable inventory and comparison status. **The live catalog is incomplete. Thirteen selected SKUs have exact manufacturer specifications, and 149 confirmed food prices are captured: 125 protein powders and 24 chocolate ice-cream candidates. The [preliminary listing inventory](../tables/discovered-listings.md) contains 2,798 distinct observed listings, kept separate from product details. Current stock, bulk freight and whole-market coverage remain unresolved.**

The [catalog audit](catalog-audit.json) records missing discovered listings, missing SKU prices, unfinished search scopes and specification gaps. [Manual review](visual-review/README.md) documents inspection of all 32 saved candidate food pages with eight committed screenshot sheets. Every displayed selected-page price matched its extracted value. [Additional SKU review](browser-sku-review/README.md) covers seven inspection sheets, 26 distinct accepted SKUs and seven rejected captures. [Factory-label reviews](manufacturer-labels/README.md) document the whey exact matches and seller corrections; the [MusaKing soy review](musa-soy-review/README.md) adds two exact Chocolate 1 kg matches and retains a conflicting serving-size illustration. Unseen products and other SKU prices remain unresolved.

One apparent frozen product and all its 29 captured variants were quarantined after its imagery showed packaged crispy cone snacks. The listing-wide correction applies to aliases, future variants and refreshed extraction. Other recorded conflicts include a vanilla title with a selected chocolate variant, a 5 lb title with a selected 1 kg repack, and inconsistent gallery quantities. Manufacturer specifications take priority only after exact identity and source authority are established; the original claims and correction history remain available.

These new captures are extraction-checked, with manual visual review still pending. [Official-source collection](manufacturer-source-crawl.json) runs independently with cached pages, label images and OCR; a source lead or product family is not an exact listing match.

[Delivery observations](delivery-result.json) use **Khánh Hòa, Phường Nha Trang** for one selected package. Unknown delivery stays unknown. Quotes do not establish the exact buyer address, stock, bulk freight or a frozen delivery chain. The calculator is available locally at http://127.0.0.1:8080 after `node bin/lazada-search.js serve`.

[Earlier seller-label reviews](review-result.json) are preserved as historical observations, including three packages with manually read nutrition. They are not current manufacturer-verified buying recommendations. ## Discovery, loading and extraction checks

Search-list collection precedes product detail collection. Every configured query remains an unfinished scope until its disabled Next control is observed. Unknown titles remain in the discovery inventory; pagination mismatches, dialogs, challenges and loading timeouts stop requests. Scrolling follows product regions in animated steps and stops at the pager, with the footer excluded. Browser E2E fixtures verify the final row, pager, footer boundary, clearance above the minimized chat control and animation frames. The [whey-search scope review](discovery-scope-review.json) records all 61 consecutive pages and 2,416 distinct observed cards; all sixteen last-page prices were visually checked. Changing UI totals are retained separately from observed card counts. Every published search card, SKU, displayed price and pagination state is re-extracted from committed redacted HTML in regression tests.

The [saved extraction audit](saved-extraction-audit.json) rechecks 142 distinct saved HTML/SKU captures with zero downloads and zero extraction errors. It records 22 captures containing loading markers for deferred recollection after the initial discovery and product passes. Visual review remains a separate requirement. [Sale discounts and promotion conditions](../tables/discounts.md) preserve original prices and vouchers without deducting displayed sale savings twice. Selected package counts and sizes control food denominators.
