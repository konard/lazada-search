# Public collection and validation

[Published catalog tables](../tables/README.md) are the current readable inventory and comparison status. **The live catalog is incomplete. Eleven selected SKUs have exact manufacturer specifications, and 140 confirmed prices are captured: 99 protein powders and 41 chocolate ice-cream candidates. Current stock, bulk freight and whole-market coverage remain unresolved.**

The [catalog audit](catalog-audit.json) records missing discovered listings, missing SKU prices, unfinished search scopes and specification gaps. [Manual review](visual-review/README.md) documents inspection of all 32 saved candidate food pages with eight committed screenshot sheets. Every displayed selected-page price matched its extracted value. [Additional SKU review](browser-sku-review/README.md) covers seven inspection sheets, 26 distinct accepted SKUs and seven rejected captures. [Factory-label reviews](manufacturer-labels/README.md) document the eleven exact matches and seller corrections. Unseen products and other SKU prices remain unresolved.

One apparent frozen product was quarantined after its imagery showed a packaged crispy cone snack. Other recorded conflicts include a vanilla title with a selected chocolate variant, a 5 lb title with a selected 1 kg repack, and inconsistent gallery quantities. Manufacturer specifications take priority only after exact identity and source authority are established; the original claims and correction history remain available.

The earlier cached catalog replay used 362 cache hits, zero downloads and zero browser starts. [Public backlog attempt](backlog-result.json) encountered an app-only page followed by security redirects. Further Lazada requests stopped. A later Chrome collection captured additional variants before reCAPTCHA blocked navigation. Subsequent Zalo sign-in succeeded in the dedicated account profile. [Authenticated publication receipts](account-publication.json) cover 94 redacted product captures, three rejected default-SKU selections and zero publication downloads. These new captures are extraction-checked, with manual visual review still pending. [Official-source collection](manufacturer-source-crawl.json) runs independently with cached pages, label images and OCR; a source lead or product family is not an exact listing match.

[Delivery observations](delivery-result.json) use **Khánh Hòa, Phường Nha Trang** for one selected package. Unknown delivery stays unknown. Quotes do not establish the exact buyer address, stock, bulk freight or a frozen delivery chain. The calculator is available locally at http://127.0.0.1:8080 after `node bin/lazada-search.js serve`.

[Earlier seller-label reviews](review-result.json) are preserved as historical observations, including three packages with manually read nutrition. They are not current manufacturer-verified buying recommendations. [Price calculations](price-comparison.json) retain every known before/after denominator and every eligibility problem; seven historical one-package offers pass the checks when stale observations are explicitly allowed. Bulk eligibility still requires its own quote.

## Test evidence

The repository includes deterministic category and coverage tests, real anonymous public cache fixtures, and end-to-end tests for Browser Commander, Tesseract, exact manufacturer evidence, quotes, cache restart, installed CLI, the browser calculator, the associative binary store and a simulated local Telegram API. Telegram tests check all 18 rows across multiple messages, incomplete-audit refusal, unauthorized users and non-private chats. Production Telegram messages are not sent by tests.

[Committed public cache fixtures](../../tests/fixtures/public-cache/README.md) include four actual products, original labels, bilingual OCR, destination quotes and full public SKU inventories in `.lino` and binary `.links`. Offline replay never starts a browser or downloads data. The full normalized [catalog snapshot](../tables/catalog.json) also imports into a fresh associative store with the same offers and coverage gaps, without website requests. Exact validation counts are recorded in [validation.json](validation.json).

Dependency reports and workarounds are tracked in [upstream-issues.md](../upstream-issues.md).
